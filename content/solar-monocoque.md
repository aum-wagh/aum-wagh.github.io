## Role: Structures Lead at UBC Solar Racing

## Skills:

{{< project-skills >}}

## Highlights:

- Led the design of a carbon fibre composite monocoque using Ansys ACP, achieving a 40% weight reduction through layup optimization and material selection across multiple design iterations 
- Developed a design workflow involving CAD modelling on SolidWorks and FEA on Ansys ACP 
- Optimized the monocoque to pass the 5G impact regulations for the American Solar Challenge, and designed around the reaction forces from the suspension and rollcage 
- Simulated a torsional stiffness test on Ansys ACP to observe deformation under a torsional load
- Executed a structural materials testing plan with tensile, 4-point bend, adhesive, joint, and hardpoint insert testing according to ASTM 
standards, with the objective of validating the monocoque designs and sub-assemblies  
- Coordinated system integration with the suspension, battery, roll cage, aeroshell, and electronics, while overseeing project timelines 
- Managed a team of four engineering students working towards component design and CAD integration, while ensuring technical progress 
within budget constraints and tracking project progress on Monday.com
