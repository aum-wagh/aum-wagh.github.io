## Role: Composites Lead at UBC Solar Racing

## Skills:

{{< project-skills >}}

## Highlights:

- Organized and led the composite layups of the large-scale aeroshell, measuring 5m x 1.3m x 1.1m
- Achieved a 40% weight reduction and 70% improvement in stiffness from the previous aeroshell, through material selection and an optimized vacuum-bagging process
- Manufactured the honeycomb sandwich panels for the carbon fibre bulkheads and fibreglass battery enclosure, reducing overall component costs by 27% with in-house prepreg layups and finishing 
- Assisted with the fibreglass mould layups, and developed a detailed plan for foam thermoforming, ply cutting, and layup consumables prep
