## Highlights:

- Designed the mounts and covers for the backup camera and headlights with SolidWorks in accordance with competition regulations for the 2024 American Solar Challenge
- Designed custom moulds for PETG thermoforming, printed all the parts with ABS, and succesfully integrated the parts in the solar car
