## Highlights:

- Collaborated with Moon and Mars Industries and a team of 6 to optimize the cure cycle of a composite heat shield using DSC, resulting in a 
46% increase in the elastic modulus and a 34% increase in glass transition temperature from the previous iteration 
- Optimized the vacuum-infusion process with closed moulding and envelop-bagging, achieving a 40% reduction in total manufacturing time
- Coordinated the logistics of lab access, equipment scheduling, and mechanical testing with the supervisors and lab technicians
