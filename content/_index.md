## About:

Hello! My name is Aum, I'm a Master's student in Materials Engineering at the University of British Columbia. My master's thesis was focused on developing FEA models to predict the mechanical properties of unidirectional composites, and validating the simulations with longitudinal tensile testing. In parallel, I was the Structures Lead at UBC Solar Racing, where I helped design the team's first composite monocoque for the fourth-generation solar-powered racecar. I have experience with R&D, materials testing, manufacturing, leadership, and I enjoy working with composites!
