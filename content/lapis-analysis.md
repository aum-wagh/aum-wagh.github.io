## Highlights:

- Applied SEM, EDS, and XRD techniques to characterize a lapis lazuli sample, with the goal of determining the phase distribution and chemical composition of the sample
- Interpreted microstructural images and compositional data to identify the presence of lazurite, wollastonite, pyrite, and diopside
- Collaborated with a team of 4 to compile a technical report and deliver a technical presentation to the project supervisor
