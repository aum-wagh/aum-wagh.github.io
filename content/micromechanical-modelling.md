## Highlights:

- Developed a Python algorithm to generate random models for unidirectional composites on Abaqus with up to 70% fibre volume fractions  
- Validated Abaqus FEA simulations with longitudinal tensile testing according to ASTM D3039, using digital image correlation (DIC) for strain measurement 
- Optimized the model parameters and inputs with a sensitivity analysis, achieving model predictions within 2.6% of the experimental results
